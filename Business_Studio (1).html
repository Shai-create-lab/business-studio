How to write a stronger prompt
  • Give it a role. "Act as a branding specialist" sets the point of view.
  • Add context. Say who the business is, where it is and who it serves.
  • Name the format. Ask for a table, a numbered list, JSON or two short paragraphs.
  • Set limits. Word counts, tone and "do not invent prices" keep answers usable.
  • Show an example when you want a particular style.
  • Iterate. Generate, refine one thing at a time, and compare versions.
