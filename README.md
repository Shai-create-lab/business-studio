Business Studio is an online platform where small business owners can post about their business to attract customers, get feedback, receive reactions, and advertise their products and services. 
. Why I Developed This 

This is why I developed Business Studio - so that users can explore all businesses in an area faster, easier, and based on real feedback. 

Finding a business is simple. Each post consists of: 

- Business name 
- Description 
- Location 
- Contacts 
- Website URL 
- Pictures of the business

- - AI-Powered Creation Tools: 

- Prompt Library: Provides ready-to-use starter prompts to help users create better posts and automatically saves every prompt you generate. 

- AI Assistant: Helps business owners generate business names, descriptions, and social media posts, with smart suggestions for names, descriptions, and more. 

 - Built-in Logo Maker: Allows business owners to create custom, professional logos for their business simply by using text prompts, according to their own liking.
